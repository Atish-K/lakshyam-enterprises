About Lakshyam Enterprises

A focused supplier of SMT and electronics assembly equipment, spare parts and consumables — with the process knowledge to help you choose well.

Who we are

Your partner for SMT and electronics assembly

Lakshyam means “target” — and our target is simple: help electronics manufacturers build and maintain productive assembly lines.

Based in Bangalore and led by Gayethri S.V (Proprietor), we supply the machines that run your process — pick and place, stencil printing, reflow and wave soldering, inspection, depaneling, through-hole insertion and board handling — together with the nozzles, feeders, spare parts and consumables that keep them running every day.

Every recommendation starts with your board, volume and quality goals. That way you invest in capability you will actually use, and see lasting gains in efficiency, cost and quality.

From a single roll of splice tape to a complete line upgrade, every enquiry gets the same attention: clear specifications, honest lead times and support after the sale.
